# TechnologyExplorerCompanyPanel

**Summary panel showing a company's context for a selected technology.**

Appears in the right column of TechnologyExplorer when the visitor selects a specific technology. It answers "how did this person use this technology at this company" with role, period and project list.

## Responsibilities

- Display company-level aggregates: total months, role count, technology count.
- List roles with period and short summary.
- Stay readable at panel width without truncating critical information.

## What it is not

Not a full company profile. Not responsible for selection state — it only renders what it receives.
