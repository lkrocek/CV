# TechnologyExplorerDetail

**Right-panel drill-down showing evidence for a selected technology.**

The detail panel is the payoff of the explorer interaction. Once a technology is selected, this component shows total exposure, matching roles and — on a second click — project-level context.

## Responsibilities

- Show aggregate stats for the selected technology.
- List company entries ordered by recency.
- Allow drilling into a specific entry to surface project details.
- Animate the transition between overview and entry state.

## What it is not

Not responsible for fetching or computing insights — it receives them as props.
