# TechnologyExplorerHeader

**Toolbar for the technology list: title, view mode, sort and group filter.**

Sits at the top of TechnologyExplorer and owns all list-level controls. Switching view mode toggles between duration bars and a timeline. The group filter delegates to SkillGroupFilter.

## Responsibilities

- Label the section and communicate the current state.
- Toggle between duration and timeline view modes.
- Toggle sort direction.
- Open the skill group filter dropdown.

## What it is not

Not responsible for applying any of the controls — it delegates everything via callbacks.
